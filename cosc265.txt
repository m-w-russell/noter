###Entities and Attributes
] *Entities* are specific objects or things in the mini-world that are represented in the database
] *Attributes* are properties used to describe an enemy
] Each attribute has a *domain* (value set)

*Types of Attributes*
] *Simple (atomic) or Composite*
]] Gender is a *simple* attribute
]] Name (FirstName, LastName) is composite
] *Single/Multi Valued*
]] PreviousDegrees of a STUDENT
]] Denoted as {PreviousDegrees}

An attribute can be a combination of these, e.g. an attribute can be *composite* and *multi-valued*. Degrees of a STUDENT is a composite multi-valued attribute denoted by {Degrees(University, Year, Degree, Field)}

*The NULL Value*
There are three possible interpretations of a *NULL* value:
] Not known
] Not applicable
] Missing

*Entity Types and Key Attributes*
An *entity type* is a group of entities with the same attributes. An attribute of an entity type for which each individual entity must have a *unique* value is called a *key attribute*, e.g. student numbers, student usernames, IRD numbers, email addresses, employee codes, etc. An entity type can have ^*more* than one^ key attribute under the EER model.


##Relationships
A relationship relates two or ore distinct *entities* with a specific meaning. e.g. ^john smith^ works on ^ProductX^. Relationships of the same *type* are grouped into a *relationship type*.
