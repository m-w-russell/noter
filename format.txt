#Formatting Info:

#Prefixes:
\# - Heading 1
\## - Heading 2
\###- Heading 3
\#### - Heading 4

\] - Bulleted List
\]] - Nested Bullet 1
\]]] - Nested bullet 2

## Displays as:
#Heading 1
##Heading 2
###Heading 3
####Heading 4

] Bullet Point
]] Nested Bullet 1
]]] Nested bullet 2

#Inline Formatting
inline \*bold\*
Becomes inline *bold*

##Shortcuts:
] F1 : Save
] F5: Render Notes
